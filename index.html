      Honest method notes

      Ranking scores are predictions of a proxy formula (0.45·similarity + 0.25·citations + 0.15·recency + 0.10·open-access + 0.05·length + 0.05·similarity×citations), learned by a Random Forest. This is weak supervision by formula, not human relevance labels. Open access ≠ quality; citations favour older work; ~50 papers is one search sample, not the full literature. The AI answer is generated text — verify against the cited papers.
